# Bloom Brainwork Studio

Create a modern task management app with a clean, minimal design. Include features for adding tasks, marking them complete, organizing by categories, and a dark mode toggle. Use a calming brown and pink color scheme. also Build a Smart Email Generator that will Generate professional emails and Support multiple tones (Formal, Friendly, Persuasive).  Meeting Notes Summarizer. The app should Summarize lengthy meeting notes and Extract action items, decisions, and deadlines.

 AI Task Planner / Scheduler . Also Generate daily or weekly schedules and Prioritise tasks effectively. AI Research Assistant. Summarise topics or articles and Provide insights and recommendations. AI Chatbot Interface. Interactive AI workplace assistant and Respond to user prompts.                                                                                                                    dont forget Modern dashboard UI,Sidebar navigation,Responsive design,Structured AI prompts,Editable AI outputs,Responsible AI disclaimer
